# Open performance issues: plan and order

Every open issue labelled performance or research on 2026-11-10 (#217 to
#228, #249 to #261), what is left in it, what it waits on, and which lever
of this round changes it. The last section lists implementation findings
met along the way that have no issue yet.

"Needs a GPU" means the work is a measurement or a gate on a CUDA build;
none of those ran in this round.
