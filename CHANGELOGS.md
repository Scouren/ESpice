# Changelog

Newest first.

## 0.20.0 (unreleased)

0.20.0 was not tagged on 2026-11-02; the entries of that date follow these, and 0.3.0, never released, is folded in below them.

- A portnum V's z0 publishes no `.dcmatch`, `.dcsens` or `.acmatch` row and leaves the total, as `.noise` and `.sens` already do. Before, each analysis added the hidden resistor's ten `resistor#0.<param>` rows (measured on a one-port divider), and its variance entered the total. The rows left are those of the same deck with the z0 written as its own R card, less that card's, and the totals are the root sum of squares of the rows left. The `.sens` and match schemas count the same rows, where `.sens`'s counted the z0's (#265).
- `--emir` writes a portnum V as one source between its n+ and n−, as ngspice's vsrc is: its z0 has no entry, and the source's p terminal sits at n+, where the z0 wrote two terminals named `resistor` and the source's p sat at `<card>#res`. Every net's currents still sum to zero (#265).
- A resistor with `noisy=0` or `noise=0` publishes no `.noise ... pts` columns, where it published `onoise_<r>_thermal` and `onoise_<r>` at zero in both plots. This matches ngspice 44.2's name list for the same deck (measured), whose resnoise.c skips a quiet resistor. The other columns and the totals are unchanged (#265).
- Two portnum V cards on one port number make one balanced port in `.sp` and `.lin`, where the second card was `DuplicatePortNumber`; ngspice 44.2 refuses the deck (`duplicate port Index`, measured). It is ESPice's spelling of HSPICE's mixed-mode P element, which SpiceRack writes as `Vp1_p a ref dc v/2 ac m/2 portnum 1 z0 50` and `Vp1_n b ref dc -v/2 ac m/2 180 portnum 1 z0 50`. The first card is the + leg, and each leg runs against its own n−. S, Y, Z and donoise's Cy are published over the differential (2·z0) and common (z0/2) modes in HSPICE's Touchstone order, and `.lin MIXEDMODE2PORT=` reads `d` and `c` again. Unequal z0 or n− is `UnbalancedPort`, a third card `DuplicatePortNumber`, and `.hblin` refuses a balanced port. The checks are the Bockelman-Eisenstadt transform of the same deck's single-ended S and Z, and `hspice/lin_mixed_mode`'s analytic oracle in both dialects (#265).
- A diode's breakdown knee follows ngspice's fixed point (diotemp.c) until its current is within reltol·IBV of IBV, at most 25 passes, where it took one pass: at `.options reltol=1e-9` the LEVEL=3 diode D4 of `dc/diode_area_sidewall`, run alone, carries 1.06643e-5 A at -8.35 V, as ngspice 45 measures, where it carried 1.06751e-5 A. At the default reltol one pass already met the test, so nothing moves there (#265).
- `.sp` publishes `v(Y_i_j)` and `v(Z_i_j)` after S, and donoise's `v(Cy_i_j)`, `v(NF)`, `v(SOpt)`, `v(NFmin)` and `v(Rn)` after Z, in ngspice 44.2's column order (measured), where it published S and the donoise columns alone. They are derived from S on the host, so a GPU lane's sweep publishes them too; the formulas give ngspice 44.2's own Y and Z from its S to 7e-16 relative on a two-port with unequal z0 (measured) (#265).
- `.sp`'s plot leads with the node voltages and branch currents of the last port's drive, each port's `v(<card>#res)` among them, ahead of S, as ngspice 44.2's plot does (measured: it drives the highest port number with 1 V and holds the other ports at 0). Touchstone and CITIfile still write S alone (#265).
- HSPICE's other spellings of ESPice cards are gone from ESPice's reader and refused with `UnsupportedCard`, as ngspice 44.2 refuses them ("unimplemented dot command", measured), and SpiceRack writes the ESPice card: `.sn` as `.pss`, `.snac` as `.pac`, `.snnoise` as `.pnoise`, `.snxf` as `.pxf v(out)`, `.snosc` as `.pss v(osc) f`, `.hbosc` as `.hb v(osc) f`, `.acphasenoise` as `.noise` (its `phnoise` is 10·log10(onoise²/2), which ESPice no longer publishes), `.power` as four `.meas tran` cards, `.stimuli` as `.stim` and `.jitter` as `.meas ... jitter`. Without `--tokenizer` each one is a marker that sends the deck to SpiceRack. `.ptdnoise` stays and runs at the fundamental of the deck's first driven `.pss`, where it took `.sn`'s. The corpus decks that used these cards now spell the ESPice card; on the build before this change the oscillator, phase-noise, `.power` and `.jitter` decks give byte-identical output in both spellings (#265).
- `.pac`, `.pxf` and `.pnoise` read their frequency grid as `.ac` does, HSPICE's `POI n f1 ... fn` included, where they read only `dec`, `oct` and `lin` (#265).

- `.pss` with an even number of steps solves circuits where a trapezoid current that no charge backs rings with multiplier +1 over the period, so the shooting Jacobian was singular. That covers a loop of capacitors and voltage sources (ngspice's Colpitts example, `error.Singular` before) and a capacitor whose two nodes hold no other charge (ngspice's Hartley example, `NoPeriodicSteadyState` at residual 8.8e7 before). The seam seed now drops the summed current of such a node pair, and the loop current's row, found from the circuit's structure, asks for no ringing on every Newton path: the monodromy's dense and GMRES solves, and the finite-difference columns and GMRES past its memory budget. A driven RLC deck whose capacitor floats between a resistor and an inductor, at 64 steps, published a 157 V zigzag on a 1 V drive as converged. On the GMRES path, a driven capacitor and voltage source loop padded past 50 unknowns published i(vs) ringing at 3.1e-3 A on a 6.3e-3 A peak as converged. Their tests pin every path to the bilinear-warped phasor solution (v(b) 0.1135029113 V on the first) and every column's Nyquist bin to zero (#265).

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-19)

- **Dialects:** Spectre netlists (`--tokenizer=spectre`); HSPICE quoting, braces, `$` comments and operators follow the HSPICE manual (#203); many ngspice parsing details match ngspice (#121).
- **New:** VPI applications (`--vpi app.so`) for Verilog-A `$systf` calls and analog callbacks (#197, #198); `.dc` over a `.param`; `.vec` files; `.qpss` on multi-tone HB; `OPTIMIZE=` grid steps; `live_params` / `set_params` to change `.param` values without re-parsing (#101).

- **GPU:** BSIM4, VBIC, MOS 1–9, sources, lines and filters evaluate on the GPU; the device LU takes large post-layout circuits and is 4x faster there (#139–#141).

- **Models:** third-party compact models (PSP, BSIM-CMG/BULK/IMG/SOI, HiSIM, HICUM, Mextram, ASM-HEMT, MVSG and more) from their upstream releases, unmodified.
- `ESPICE_THREADS` gives the same output at any thread count (#137).
